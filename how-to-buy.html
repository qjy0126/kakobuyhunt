How to buy from Kakobuy

Last updated September 20, 2026. Kakobuy Hunt is a free spreadsheet catalog. You open a find here, then pay, QC, and ship on Kakobuy.

  1. How do I find a product on Kakobuy Hunt?

    Open a category or use search — every card already has a working Kakobuy link with a USD price.

  2. How do I open the item on Kakobuy?

    Click View on Kakobuy and create a free account if you need one — you need it to order and to see warehouse QC later.

  3. How do I choose size, colour and version?

    Sizing is generally true to size. Check the listing chart if you are between sizes, and leave measurements in the agent note when needed.

  4. How do I place the order and pay?

    Checkout covers the product itself. International shipping is quoted later — if PayPal is not offered, top up your Kakobuy balance.

  5. How do I track warehouse stages?

    Orders move Process Pending → Purchased → Seller Sent → Warehouse Received. QC photos appear after warehouse check-in.

  6. How do I review QC photos?

    Inspect stitching, logos, materials and colour on the exact unit you will receive. Raise issues before you approve shipping.

  7. How do I choose shipping?

    Approve the item, pick a shipping line, and use the estimator. Removing shoeboxes and consolidating a haul usually cuts the bill.

  8. How do I receive the parcel?

    Kakobuy packs, hands off to the line you picked, and gives you tracking. Delivery time depends on that line.
