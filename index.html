What is Kakobuy?

Kakobuy is a Chinese shopping agent (a buying agent / proxy). Instead of selling anything itself, it buys items for you, stores them in its warehouse, lets you inspect them with warehouse QC photos, and then forwards everything to your country in one parcel. Most sellers will not ship internationally or accept foreign cards — the agent removes that friction.

Shoppers care most about quality control. When your item reaches the Kakobuy warehouse, staff photograph the exact unit you will receive — stitching, logos, materials, colour. You review those QC photos before paying for international shipping.

What is a Kakobuy Spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy Spreadsheet is a free curated catalog of finds with photos, USD prices, and direct Kakobuy checkout links. Kakobuy Hunt lists 4000+ items across shoes, hoodies, jackets and more. This site never holds stock or payments.

Is the spreadsheet free to use?

Yes — browsing every find and link on Kakobuy Hunt is free, with no account, signup, or paywall.

Is Kakobuy safe and legit?

Yes — Kakobuy is an established Chinese shopping agent that buys on your behalf, holds items in a warehouse, and photographs them for QC before international shipping. Kakobuy Hunt only links out and does not process payments. Always review QC photos before you approve shipment.

How much does Kakobuy shipping cost?

It depends on weight, dimensions, destination, and the line you choose. Use Kakobuy’s estimator once items are in the warehouse — removing bulky packaging and consolidating into one parcel usually lowers the total.

How long does delivery take?

Usually about 7–20 days after dispatch, depending on the shipping line. Express is faster and costs more; economy is cheaper and slower. Customs and holidays can add time.
